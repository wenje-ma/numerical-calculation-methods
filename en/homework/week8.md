> **Definition 8.2 (Order of Convergence)**<br>$\lim|e_{n+1}|/|e_n|^r=a$.

> **Theorem 8.2 (Convergence Condition)**<br>$|\varphi'|\le L<1$.

> **Theorem 8.4 (Order and Derivatives)**<br>$r$-th order when first $r-1$ derivatives vanish.
