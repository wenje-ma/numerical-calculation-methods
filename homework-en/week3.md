> **Definition 3.3 (Spectral Radius)**
> 
> $\rho\left(A\right)=\max\left|\lambda_i\right|$.

> **Definition 3.4 (Diagonally Dominant Matrix)**
> 
> $\left|a_{ii}\right|\ge\sum_{j\ne i}\left|a_{ij}\right|$.

> **Theorem 3.6 (Convergence Criterion)**
> 
> $\rho\left(M\right)< 1$.
